ROHIT GURUNATH SHARMA

Rohit Gurunath Sharma is an iconic Indian international cricketer, widely celebrated as 'Hitman' for his explosive and elegant batting style. Born on April 30, 1987, he is a right-handed opening batsman and one of the most successful captains in modern cricket history. He has a world record of scoring highest runs(264) in an odi and he also smashes a '3' double hundred in one day internationals
